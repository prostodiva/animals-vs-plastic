Frequently Asked Questions

Animals mistake plastic for food and eat it, which can block their stomachs and starve them. Others get tangled in bags, nets, and fishing line and cannot swim, hunt, or surface to breathe. Plastic also damages the habitats animals depend on - from coral reefs to nesting beaches.

Plastic does not biodegrade. A plastic bottle can last around 450 years in the ocean. Sunlight and waves only break it into smaller and smaller pieces - it never truly goes away.

Microplastics are pieces of plastic smaller than 5 millimeters. They form when larger items break down, and they also wash off clothing and out of cosmetics. Small sea creatures eat them, and they move up the food chain - all the way to our plates.

Start with small choices: use fewer single-use plastics, reuse bags, bottles, and containers, dispose of plastic responsibly, pick up plastic when you see it, and think before you buy. Multiplied by millions of people, small choices become an ocean-sized change - you can take the pledge on our home page.
