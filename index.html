THE PROBLEM

Plastic Doesn't Just Disappear

Every year, millions of tons of plastic flow from our cities and rivers into the ocean. Sunlight and waves don't destroy it — they shatter it into smaller and smaller pieces.

8M+ tons of plastic enter the ocean every year
52% of sea turtles have ingested plastic debris
100K+ marine animals die from entanglement each year
2050 the year plastic could outweigh fish in the ocean

What you can do

Small choices, multiplied by millions of people, become an ocean-sized change.

↓
  1. Use fewer single-use plastics.
  2. Reuse bags, bottles, and containers.
  3. Dispose of plastic responsibly.
  4. Pick up plastic when you see it.
  5. Think before you buy.
